About Mify

Mify is an integrated health intelligence platform that connects data collection, AI analysis, and intelligent agents to solve critical healthcare bottlenecks. From affordable Arduino monitoring to advanced AI insights, we create a seamless ecosystem that democratizes health analytics.

Solving Healthcare's Biggest Challenges

Healthcare faces critical bottlenecks: fragmented data across devices, expensive monitoring solutions, complex analysis requirements, and time-constrained providers. These barriers prevent effective health monitoring and personalized care delivery.

Mify's integrated ecosystem addresses these challenges through a connected platform that combines affordable Arduino monitoring, Google Fit integration, AI-powered analysis with Fetch.ai's ASI:One, and intelligent agents. This creates a seamless workflow from data collection to actionable insights, democratizing health analytics for all communities.

Affordable Health Monitoring

Build your own health monitoring system with Arduino and ultrasonic sensors. A cost-effective solution that democratizes health tracking for communities worldwide, powered by AI analysis and real-time insights.

Hardware Setup

Arduino UNO R4 WiFi

Advanced IoT board with ESP32-S3, 12x8 LED matrix, Qwiic connector, and WiFi/Bluetooth

Ultrasonic Sensor (HC-SR04)

Measures distance and detects movement for health parameter monitoring

Hue Light Indicator

Visual feedback system that activates when placed on conductive surfaces

Metal Tin Plate

Conductive surface that completes the circuit for light activation
