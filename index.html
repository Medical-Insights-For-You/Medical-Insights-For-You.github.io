About Mify

Mify is an integrated health intelligence platform that connects data collection, AI analysis, and intelligent agents into a seamless workflow that benefits both patients and providers.

Our Mission

We're democratizing healthcare access by making advanced health monitoring and AI-powered insights available to everyone, regardless of their economic situation or location.

Integrated Solution

Mify combines multiple health data sources - from high-end wearables to affordable Arduino sensors - and processes them through our AI ecosystem to provide actionable health insights.

Technology Stack
